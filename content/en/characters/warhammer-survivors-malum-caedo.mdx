## Who Is Malum Caedo? Lore and Background

Malum Caedo is a Sternguard Veteran of the Ultramarines, a member of the Chapter's First Company — the veterans who get deployed when a problem needs to be removed rather than managed. He reached a wide audience in *Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun*, where he was dispatched to the forge world of Graia to blunt a Chaos incursion essentially on his own. His defining traits are consistent: relentless forward pressure, a heavy volume of bolt-weapon fire, and a steady stream of dry one-liners delivered while surrounded by daemons.

That lore translates neatly into a survivors-style game. The genre is built around constant motion, escalating enemy density, and upgrades that snowball — and a character whose whole identity is "advance and keep firing" fits it almost too well. You are not playing a schemer or a support piece. You are playing the guy the Imperium sends when it wants a problem solved loudly.

## How to Build Malum Caedo

Building him well is less about finding a secret combination and more about respecting a priority order. Damage first, coverage second, survivability third, comfort stats last. Players who invert that order tend to have spectacular early runs and short ones.

| Priority | Upgrade Category | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Tactics, Positioning, and Common Mistakes

Positioning wins runs in this genre, and **Warhammer Survivors Malum Caedo** is no exception. Your ideal state is always "firing while moving away from the biggest threat." Circling wide beats zig-zagging in place, because wide arcs give you more time between contact and more chances to funnel enemies into a line.

- Keep firing on the move — stopping to finish a kill is how most runs end.
- Fight in open space when you can, and use terrain to split packs rather than to hide.
- Spend pickups and level-ups immediately; banked power is dead power.
- Treat elite enemies as the only real target priority; let area damage handle the chaff.

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | The Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing still to confirm a kill | Enemies surround you for free | Fire while retreating |
| Hoarding level-ups for a "perfect" build | You lose tempo you never get back | Spend early, adapt often |
| Chasing stragglers across the map | You abandon your safe loop | Let area damage finish them |
| Ignoring elite enemies | They stack up into an unwinnable wave | Reprioritize the moment one appears |
| Full glass-cannon build | One mistake ends the run | Take at least one defensive option |

## Co-op and Team Synergy

In co-op, he is the pressure valve. He pushes forward, thins the crowd, and creates the space that slower or squishier teammates need to function. What he does not do well is babysit — so pair him with characters who can lock enemies down while he converts that crowd into a cleared screen.

| Partner Role | Why It Works With Him |
| --- | --- |
| Crowd control | Holds enemies inside his firing lanes |
| Melee anchor | Keeps packs off his flank while he shoots |
| Support / buffer | Amplifies the damage he is already stacking |
| Second ranged damage | Doubles the rate the screen gets cleared |

Good co-op habits are boring and effective: call out which direction you are pushing, do not drag a train of enemies through a teammate's safe zone, and revive only when the area around the downed player is genuinely clear. Player experience suggests most co-op wipes come from a rescue attempt, not from the wave itself.

## Tips From Player Experience

These are the habits that show up again and again in community discussions about **Warhammer Survivors Malum Caedo**. None of them are flashy, and all of them matter.

- Decide your build direction in the first few levels, then commit — split builds underperform.
- Learn the spawn rhythm of a map before chasing high scores on it.
- Prioritize area coverage over raw single-target damage once hordes thicken.
- Keep one escape option available at all times; never spend your last mobility tool aggressively.
- Practice "kiting in circles" until it is automatic; it is the single biggest survivability upgrade.
- Re-evaluate your build every few levels instead of forcing a plan that is not being offered.

| Run Phase | Main Goal | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Mid-run | Scale area damage | Coverage, fire rate, tempo |
| Late run | Survive damage spikes | Survivability, positioning, target priority |

## FAQ

**Is Malum Caedo a good character in Warhammer Survivors?**
He is a strong, forgiving pick for most players, especially anyone who prefers aggressive ranged play over careful setup. Community reports describe him as easy to start with and rewarding to master, though he demands movement discipline once elite enemies appear.

**Do I need to play *Boltgun* to understand him?**
Not at all. Knowing his background as an Ultramarines Sternguard Veteran adds flavor, but his in-game identity is simple: sustained fire, forward pressure, and a lot of dead cultists. The lore is a bonus, not a prerequisite.

**What is the best build for Warhammer Survivors Malum Caedo?**
There is no single best build, but the reliable shape is damage first, area coverage second, survivability third. Add movement speed and utility only after the core is online, and stay flexible about what the level-up options actually give you.

**Is he worth using in co-op?**
Yes — he is one of the better front-line damage dealers to bring alongside crowd-control or support characters. Just remember that his job is to clear space, not to play medic, so let teammates handle rescues when the area is still dangerous.

Whether you are chasing a longer run or just want a character whose answer to every problem is more bolt rounds, **Warhammer Survivors Malum Caedo** delivers exactly what his reputation promises. Keep moving, keep shooting, and let the horde come to you.
