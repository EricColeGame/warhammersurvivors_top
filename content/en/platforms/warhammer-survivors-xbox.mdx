## Tips for a Better Warhammer Survivors Xbox Experience

Once you are in, small adjustments make a noticeable difference. Survivors-likes reward readability above almost everything else, and console defaults do not always prioritize that.

- Prioritize frame rate over resolution if the game offers a display mode toggle.
- Turn off motion blur so fast-moving swarms stay legible.
- Reduce vibration for long sessions; heavy rumble gets tiring after an hour.
- Test two or three characters before committing to one main.
- Quit properly before closing the app if the game syncs online progress.
- Keep the console ventilated, especially during 30-minute runs with heavy effects.

| Setting | Recommended starting point | Why |
